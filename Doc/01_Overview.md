# Resume Studio — overview

Resume Studio compares a pasted job description (JD) with a pasted or attached resume. It then shows:

- current ATS-style **score**
- **matching** keywords
- **missing** keywords
- **why the resume may not be selected**

One click writes a **tailored resume** that keeps the experience already in your document, then updates the score and matching keywords. You choose **Save PDF** or **Save Word**.

There is **no URL import**. Paste the JD and resume (or attach a `.txt` / `.html` file).

Live site: https://venkat0718.github.io/resume-studio/

Local: `python app.py` then http://127.0.0.1:5055
